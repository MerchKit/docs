# Pre-export validation checklist

Before sending a new assortment or updating a live store, review a small sample. This is a manual checklist, not a promise that Merchkit has automatically checked every required field or marketplace rule.

## Prepare a sample

1. Confirm the destination connection and whether it targets a test or production environment.
2. Review the export mapping or channel configuration. Make sure the intended source attributes map to the destination fields.
3. Select a few representative products, including a variant and a product with optional values missing where relevant.
4. Check product identifiers so updates reach the correct existing records.

You need `data.export` to export. Ask the person who manages the integration to review its configuration if you cannot access it.

## Review the values

- **Required information:** check the destination's requirements for the specific product class. Do not treat a short universal checklist as a complete list of required fields.
- **Numbers and units:** compare dimensions, weight and prices with the supplier information. For example, confirm whether `24` means inches or centimeters.
- **Select values:** use the options accepted for that attribute and category.
- **AI-written claims:** inspect the supplier evidence for material, safety and other factual claims. A confidence rating or citation is not a guarantee; see [Reviewing AI content](/product/attributes-and-enrichment/reviewing-ai-content).
- **Images:** check that the intended images are linked and their URLs are usable. For Wayfair creates, images must be in the attributes used by the channel export.
- **Selection and mappings:** hiding a column is not a way to remove it from an integration mapping. A filter can stop matching products after values change.

## Export and verify

1. [Export the sample](/product/channels-and-content-generation/exporting-channel-data) through the intended connection.
2. Read the completed job's success and error counts.
3. Check the resulting records in the destination. An accepted Wayfair submission can take additional time to appear.
4. [Resolve rejected values](/product/channels-and-content-generation/feed-validation-errors) and retest before exporting the larger batch.

A completed job or an error-free row is not proof that every product is live or meets all marketplace rules.
