# Exporting channel data

After updating a seasonal collection's tags or preparing a new assortment, choose how to send the data. A connected integration sends records to a platform. A CSV or TSV export downloads a file; downloading it does not publish products.

## Before you start

- Your workspace must have the destination integration connected and configured. Review its [export mapping](/product/integrations-and-field-mapping/setting-up-export-mapping) before writing to a live store.
- You need export permission (`data.export`). Editing product values and integration settings requires their respective permissions.
- Check which products and fields you intend to send. Test a small selection before a large update.
- For Wayfair, confirm the intended environment, supplier configuration, product class and channel attributes with your integration administrator. Do not assume a test connection writes to production.

Use the [pre-export checklist](/product/channels-and-content-generation/pre-export-validation) to review the data. Exporting can update live platform records; a successful Merchkit job does not guarantee marketplace acceptance or storefront visibility.

## Export a Shopify tag update

Suppose you have reviewed a collection's seasonal tags and want to send them to your connected store.

1. Open **Products** or the relevant channel grid. Check the product identities and the tag values you intend to send.
2. Review the Shopify export mapping and choose the intended template if your workspace has several. Hiding a column in the grid does not remove it from an integration mapping.
3. Select a small group of products with the row checkboxes.
4. Click **Export** in the table toolbar, then choose your Shopify connection's name. If it has multiple export templates, choose the intended template from its submenu.
5. Follow the job progress and inspect any errors. Check the same products in Shopify to confirm the tags and other mapped data are correct.
6. Once the sample is correct, select the intended larger batch and repeat the export.

The export workflow handles batching, parallel work and Shopify rate limits; there is no speed setting to enable. Duration varies with the number of records, mapped data and channel limits. The timing example in the [changelog](/changelog) is one pre-release test, not a completion-time guarantee.

Only select-all Shopify exports use the current filter model to resolve the selected set. Review the selection count and any exclusions before starting; a saved view is not a permanent list of products. Do not assume an export sends only cells changed since the previous run.

### Shopify troubleshooting

- **The connection is missing from Export:** select products first, then check the connection and your export permission.
- **Unintended fields changed:** review the selected mapping template. Removing a column from view does not remove its export mapping.
- **An image did not export:** images without a usable URL are skipped rather than failing product creation. Fix the source image URL before retrying.
- **An export is taking longer than expected:** check job progress and channel errors before starting a duplicate run. Larger payloads and rate limits affect duration.

## Submit products to Wayfair

1. Open the relevant product or Wayfair channel grid and review a small sample's category, supplier part number, values and images.
2. Select the individual product rows to send. Use an explicit selection for this workflow rather than assuming a select-all filter will be interpreted like Shopify's.
3. Click the toolbar's **Export**, then choose your Wayfair connection's name.
4. Follow job progress. The export determines whether to create or update each product from its saved Wayfair identity. Do not clear that identity simply to retry an update.
5. Read the completed job's exported and error counts. Inspect the **Status** column for rejected products and follow [Resolving export errors](/product/channels-and-content-generation/feed-validation-errors).
6. After acceptance, verify the product in Wayfair. Its processing and visibility can lag behind the Merchkit job.

For new products, required images are included in the initial create submission. Provide valid image URLs in the channel's mapped image attributes before exporting. Multi-select answers are sent as separate ranked values; use the attribute's supported options rather than inventing a joined value.

Submission IDs and statuses are retained for tracing while Wayfair processes the request. This is not a promise of immediate marketplace visibility or a separate customer-facing submission-history screen. If a request is accepted but the product is still missing, provide the job and product identifier to support for tracing before repeatedly creating it again.

A job marked completed can contain rejected records. Read its result counts; reaching 100% means the workflow finished, not that every product became a live listing. Successful retries clear previous export errors for the products that pass.

## Download CSV or TSV

1. Open the grid and review the attributes you want to export.
2. For specific rows, select them and choose **Export → Export Selected → CSV** or **TSV**. The selected-row menu includes its count.
3. To export the current filtered set, use **Export → Export All → CSV** or **TSV**. Check the filters before starting.
4. Follow the export job and retrieve its output. Inspect the row count, headers and a few values before using the file elsewhere.

TSV separates columns with tabs; tabs and line breaks inside a value become spaces. A downloaded catalog file is not guaranteed to meet every marketplace's upload template. Check the destination's current requirements before importing it there.

For data corrections, see [Resolving export errors](/product/channels-and-content-generation/feed-validation-errors). For mapping changes, see [Setting up export mapping](/product/integrations-and-field-mapping/setting-up-export-mapping).
